Privacy Policy

Hunter's Networking — last updated 12 October 2026.

Hunter's Networking has no account system, no backend server, and no analytics. This page explains, plainly, what the small number of network requests the app makes actually involve.

The short version

  • Nothing you type or that the app discovers is sent to me (the developer) or stored on any server I operate — there isn't one.
  • No ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no tracking SDKs of any kind.
  • No account or sign-in, so there's no profile of you to collect in the first place.
  • Each tool only talks to the internet when you actively use it, and only sends the address you're looking up — never anything else on your device.

What each tool sends, and to whom

Hunter's Networking is a toolkit of independent network-diagnostic tools. When you use one that needs the internet, it makes a direct request from your device to the relevant public service below — there is no intermediary server of mine in between.

Each of those is operated by its own provider and governed by its own privacy policy, not mine — I don't control what they do with a request once it reaches them, only what Hunter's Networking sends.

What never leaves your device

  • Local network scanning (Network Scanner, Bonjour Browser) — stays on your local network; nothing is sent over the internet.
  • Wi-Fi details — your network's name and BSSID are read locally (iOS requires Location permission for this) and shown only to you.
  • Bluetooth scanning — nearby BLE devices are discovered locally and never transmitted anywhere.
  • Calendar reminders — SSL certificate expiry reminders are written directly to your own device calendar; nothing is uploaded.
  • Query history and favourites — stored locally on your device only.

Children's privacy

Hunter's Networking is a technical utility, not directed at children, and collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If what the app sends or asks for ever changes, this page will be updated and the "last updated" date above will change accordingly.

This policy only describes app behaviour at the last updated date above — if you're reading this much later, check that date is recent relative to the version you're using.
